Welcome.

Sunday is a personal AI you self-host. There's one chat between you and her — across the desktop app, iMessage, voice calls, and any computer you give her as a satellite.

This setup takes ninety seconds. We'll pick which Sunday daemon you're connecting to, grant microphone access for "Hey Sunday," then drop you into the chat.

Which Sunday are you connecting to?

The Sunday daemon is her central nervous system — chat history, memory, the brain. It can live on your laptop, on a VPS you control, or anywhere else you've installed her.

Microphone access

Sunday listens for "Hey Sunday" via your microphone and runs voice transcription locally. Audio never leaves your Mac unless you've configured a speech-to-text provider.
