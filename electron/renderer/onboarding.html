Welcome.

Sunday is a personal AI you self-host. There's one chat between you and her — across the desktop app, iMessage, voice calls, your browser, and any computer you give her as a satellite.

This setup takes a couple of minutes. We'll pick which Sunday daemon you're connecting to, grant microphone access for voice, hand her your browser, then drop you into the chat.

Where does Sunday live?

Sunday's brain — chat, memory, everything — runs entirely on this Mac by default. Nothing about you goes to anyone else's server. (You can point her at a daemon you've hosted elsewhere if you prefer.)

Give her a brain.

Two ways to run Sunday's thinking. Fully local keeps everything — every word, every thought — on this Mac, powered by Google's Gemma models. No keys, no accounts, nothing leaves the machine. Or bring an OpenRouter key for cloud models.

Give Sunday a browser.

This is the big one. Sunday can drive your real browser — already logged in as you — to click, fill forms, pull something off a page, book the thing, find the link. Not a sandbox, not a scraper. Your actual Chrome, doing what you'd do.
